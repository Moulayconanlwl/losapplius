# LosApplius — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what information LosApplius ("the Service") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** Your name, email address and a securely hashed password when you sign up.

**Connected social media accounts.** When you connect an account (for example TikTok), we receive, through the platform's official login, the information you authorize:

- your basic profile: account ID, display name, username and profile picture;
- access and refresh tokens that let the Service publish on your behalf.

For TikTok, the Service requests the scopes `user.info.basic`, `user.info.profile`, `video.upload` and `video.publish`.

**Content you create.** Posts, captions, schedules and media files (images and videos) that you upload in order to publish them.

We do not collect your social media passwords, and we do not read your private messages.

## 2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide the Service:

- to sign you in and keep your account secure;
- to show which accounts you have connected;
- to upload and publish the content you choose, at the time you schedule it;
- to show the status of your posts.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Sharing

Your content and the necessary access tokens are sent only to the social media platform you choose to publish to (for example TikTok), through that platform's official API. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where required by law.

## 4. Storage and security

Data is stored in the Service's database and file storage, on servers operated for the Service. Access tokens are used only to make requests to the platform they belong to. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

## 5. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. When you disconnect a social media account, its tokens are removed from the Service. You can delete your account at any time, which deletes your data from the Service. You can also revoke LosApplius's access from your TikTok (or other platform) settings at any time.

## 6. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows when it last changed.

## 8. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: open an issue at <https://github.com/Moulayconanlwl/losapplius/issues>.
